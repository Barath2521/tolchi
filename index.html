The Mark

Pressed by hand, not printed.

Before it leaves the bench, every piece is branded with the Tolchi stamp — heated, weighted, and pressed into the hide by hand. It's the same mark tanners have used for generations to sign their work: proof that a person, not a machine, stood behind it.

We work in full-grain leather because it ages the way leather is supposed to — darker, softer, more yours with every year you carry it.
